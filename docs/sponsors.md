# Sponsorship

OpenBench AI is free and open source under the MIT licence. If it is useful to you, you can support its development through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/lakshaychhaba0001-sys). Sponsorship pays for real-model testing, careful review of contributions, and work on the [roadmap](https://github.com/lakshaychhaba0001-sys/openbench-ai/blob/main/ROADMAP.md).

## How sponsorship works

- **Independence.** Sponsorship never influences OpenBench AI's statistics, results, rankings or defaults, and the project never promotes a sponsor's model.
- **Priority means earlier review.** Some tiers have their issues reviewed first. That is not a guaranteed fix, feature or deadline, and it is not a support contract.
- **Credit.** Sponsors are thanked in the release notes and, at higher tiers, in the README, using their GitHub name and profile link. The tiers are listed on the [Sponsors page](https://github.com/sponsors/lakshaychhaba0001-sys).
- **Right to decline.** A name, logo or link that is misleading or inappropriate may be declined or removed.
- **Prices.** Tier prices may change for new sponsors as the project grows. Existing sponsors keep their tier and price.

Contributions help just as much: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/lakshaychhaba0001-sys/openbench-ai/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [Discussions](https://github.com/lakshaychhaba0001-sys/openbench-ai/discussions).
